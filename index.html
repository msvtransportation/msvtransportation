06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

The most common things drivers ask before they sign. If yours isn't here, call dispatch — we pick up.

Most qualified drivers go from first call to orientation in under a week. Apply online or call dispatch — if your paperwork is in order, we move fast.
Valid CDL-A, six months minimum verifiable OTR experience (one year for our long lanes), clean MVR, and the ability to pass DOT physical and drug screening.
All 48 contiguous states, with dedicated lanes in the Midwest, Southeast, and Texas triangle. Regional drivers stay within roughly 500 miles of home.
Weekly settlements via direct deposit, mileage rates above the regional average, plus performance bonuses (clean inspection, fuel, on-time). No held-back pay, no chargebacks.
Late-model Freightliner Cascadia tractors with APUs, inverters, fridges, and premium bunks. Trailers are 53′ dry van or temperature-controlled reefer, maintained in-house.
Regional drivers home every weekend. OTR drivers two weeks out, two to three days home. We respect what's on the calendar — birthdays, anniversaries, doctor's appointments. Tell us and we route around it.
We run a paid orientation and finishing program for drivers with under one year of experience. Trainers are our top-rated drivers — paid extra for the role, not assigned to it.
CSA scores well below industry average across BASICs. Annual safety audits in-house, plus quarterly driver-led reviews. Safety isn't a poster on the wall — it's how we route.
